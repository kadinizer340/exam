Core features

Built for accurate, organized, and faster exam preparation.

Excel Student Upload

Upload student workbooks by year and section so records are ready for seating generation.

Timetable Processing

Automatic Seat Allocation

Generate classroom seating plans based on selected rooms and available seat capacity.

Subject Separation
