Formatting rules

Keep timetable files consistent.

  • Use .xls or .xlsx only.
  • Each sheet tab should match a branch mapping such as cs or me.
  • Every sheet must contain date and subject columns exactly.
  • Use real Excel date cells whenever possible so exam dates are read correctly.
  • The timetable sheets are later mapped back to student sections during seat generation.
